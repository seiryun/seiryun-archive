BIOGRAPHY

A name received. A path chosen.

Born on Saisio III, among the secluded Achura, Jat Sing was raised in traditions of meditation and spiritual cultivation.

His early years were spent studying the nature of existence, mastering the self and seeking the hidden order behind the universe. His journey eventually led him beyond the Caldari State and into the Amarr Empire, where he encountered the teachings of the Imperial faith.

His discipline and unusual interpretation of the Scriptures attracted the attention of an Imperial spiritual authority, who bestowed upon him the name Seiryun.

Jat Sing accepted the name as an honour, not as a bond of servitude.

He studies the Amarr doctrine, respects its traditions and finds profound meaning in its ancient teachings. Yet his understanding of the Way remains his own.

Neither Imperial loyalist nor Caldari servant, he travels New Eden as a Rogue Capsuleer, commanding Amarr vessels and pursuing a path of cultivation beyond the boundaries of empire.

His birth name is Jat Sing. His bestowed name is Seiryun. His path belongs to no throne.
