02 / PHILOSOPHY

THE WAY

Stillness is not surrender.

Seiryun's path is shaped by contemplation, self-discipline and the search for the underlying order of existence.

He finds value in the Amarr tradition without surrendering his independence to its institutions. To study a teaching is not necessarily to become its servant; respect does not require obedience.

不争 · Bù zhēng

Not contending. Not making a contest of every encounter. Strength need not announce itself.

无为 · Wúwéi

Effortless action. Acting in accordance with the nature of things rather than forcing the world to follow one's will.

The cultivator's principle

He does not reject the world's praise. He simply has no need of it. Titles and recognition may follow a life, but they do not define its direction.

The Way is walked, not displayed.
