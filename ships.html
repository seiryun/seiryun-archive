SHIPS

Instruments of a chosen path.

Seiryun's preference for Amarr vessels is both aesthetic and philosophical. He is drawn to their solemn architecture, gold-and-red visual language and disciplined use of energy weaponry.

His choice of ships does not make him an Imperial loyalist. A vessel can be studied, admired and flown without surrendering one's independence.
